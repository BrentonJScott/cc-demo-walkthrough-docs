# Adjust a recipe's servings

Use the **−** and **+** controls next to **Ingredients** to change servings. Ingredient amounts in **Details** update to match, and **Add to shopping list** adds those scaled amounts.

**Demo URL:** https://demo-angular-22-wgdt.free.ccpve.co.za

## Steps

### Step 1 — Sign in and pick a recipe

1. Open the demo URL and sign in (any **Email** and **Password**; blue **Log in** link in the form card).
2. Under **ALL RECIPES**, click a recipe, for example **Spaghetti carbonara**.

The **Details** panel shows the recipe name and an ingredient list.

![Recipe selected in Details](/assets/guides/adjust-recipe-servings/step-1-details.png)

### Step 2 — Find the servings controls

In **Details**, under **INGREDIENTS**, look at the right side of the heading row. You see:

- A **−** button (outline style)
- A number showing the current servings count
- A **+** button (outline style)

The **Serves** badge above the ingredients also reflects the current count.

![Servings stepper next to Ingredients](/assets/guides/adjust-recipe-servings/step-2-stepper.png)

### Step 3 — Increase or decrease servings

Click **+** to serve more people, or **−** to serve fewer ( **−** is disabled at 1 serving).

Watch the amounts in the ingredient list change (for example **400 g** spaghetti becomes a larger amount when servings go from 4 to 5).

![Scaled ingredient amounts](/assets/guides/adjust-recipe-servings/step-3-scaled-amounts.png)

### Step 4 — Add scaled items to the shopping list

Click the blue **Add to shopping list** button at the bottom of **Details**.

On **Shopping list**, the new rows in **Items** use the **scaled** amounts you saw in Step 3 (not the default amounts for the recipe's original serving size).

![Shopping list with scaled amounts](/assets/guides/adjust-recipe-servings/step-4-shopping-list.png)
