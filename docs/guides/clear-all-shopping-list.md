# Clear all items from your shopping list

Use **Clear all** when you want an empty list without removing rows one at a time.

**Demo URL:** https://demo-angular-22-wgdt.free.ccpve.co.za

## Before you start

You must be signed in and on the **Shopping list** page. **Clear all** only appears when **Items** has at least one row.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Sign in and open Shopping list

1. Open the demo URL. On the **Log in** page, type any **Email** and **Password** (the demo does not validate them).
2. Click the blue **Log in** link inside the white card (not the small **Log in** tab in the header).
3. Click **Shopping list** in the top header.

You should see the **Shopping list** title and the **Items** section.

![Shopping list with Items rows](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-1-shopping-list.png)

### Step 2 — Find Clear all

Under **ITEMS**, look to the right of the small caps **ITEMS** label. When the list is not empty, a **Clear all** button appears there (ghost style, to the right of the heading).

![Clear all next to Items heading](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-2-clear-all-visible.png)

### Step 3 — Clear the list

Click **Clear all**.

**Success looks like this:**

- Every row in **Items** disappears.
- You see the message: **Your shopping list is empty.**

![Empty shopping list](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-3-empty.png)

## Tips

- **Clear all** removes every row. To delete one row only, use the trash **Remove** icon on that row (see [Remove an item from your shopping list](remove-from-shopping-list.md)).
- The **Clear** button under **ADD ITEM** only clears the add form, not **Items**.
