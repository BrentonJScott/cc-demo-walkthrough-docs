# Clear all items from your shopping list

Use **Clear all** when you want an empty **Items** list without deleting rows one at a time.

**Demo URL:** https://demo-angular-22-wgdt.free.ccpve.co.za

## Steps

### Step 1 — Sign in

1. Open the demo URL. You see the **Log in** page.
2. Type any **Email** and **Password** (the demo does not check them).
3. Click the blue **Log in** link **inside the white card** (not the small **Log in** link in the top-right header).

You land on **Recipe book** (the **Recipes** tab in the header is highlighted).

![Log in page](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-1-login.png)

### Step 2 — Open Shopping list

Click **Shopping list** in the header.

You see **ITEMS** (small caps heading). If there is at least one row, a **Clear all** button appears on the right above the table.

![Shopping list with Clear all visible](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-2-clear-all-button.png)

### Step 3 — Clear every row

Click **Clear all**.

**Success looks like this:**

- **ITEMS** has no rows.
- The message **Your shopping list is empty.** appears in the table area.
- The **Clear all** button is hidden until you add items again.

![Empty shopping list after Clear all](/assets/guides/clear-all-shopping-list/step-3-empty-list.png)

## Tips

- **Clear all** only affects **Items**. It does not clear the **Ingredient** and **Amount** fields under **ADD ITEM** (use **Clear** there instead).
- To remove a single row, see [Remove an item from your shopping list](remove-from-shopping-list.md).
