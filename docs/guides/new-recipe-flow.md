# New recipe wizard flow

**New recipe** opens a four-step flow with a progress list, **Back**, **Next** (or **Skip** / **Done** where noted), and **Cancel** (returns to **Recipe book**).

**Demo URL:** https://demo-angular-22-wgdt.free.ccpve.co.za

The first step (recipe fields) is documented in [Add a new recipe](add-new-recipe.md).

## Steps

### Step 1 — Add recipe (progress: Add recipe)

1. Sign in and click **New recipe** on **Recipe book**.
2. Confirm the progress row shows four labels with visible text: **Add recipe**, **Shopping list**, **Rate**, **Share** (numbers **1**–**4** beside each).
3. Fill **Name** (required), **Description**, **Time**, **Serves**, and **Ingredients**, then click **Next**.

![Step 1 with progress bar and form](/assets/guides/new-recipe-flow/step-1-progress.png)

### Step 2 — Shopping list

On **Step 2 of 4**, read **Add ingredients to your shopping list**. Tick checkboxes for ingredients you want, or click **Skip** to continue without adding.

Use **Back** to return to the form, or **Next** / **Skip** to continue.

![Shopping list step with checkboxes](/assets/guides/new-recipe-flow/step-2-shopping-list.png)

### Step 3 — Rate

On **Step 3 of 4**, the heading is **How would you rate** followed by your recipe name. Click star buttons **1**–**5**, optionally enter **Note (optional)**, then **Next** or **Skip**.

The progress label for this step reads **Rate** (not “Rate a recipe”).

![Rate step with stars and optional note](/assets/guides/new-recipe-flow/step-3-rate.png)

### Step 4 — Share

On **Step 4 of 4**, the heading is **Share** followed by your recipe name. Use **Copy to clipboard**, then **Done** to return to **Recipe book** with your new recipe selected.

![Share step with copy and Done](/assets/guides/new-recipe-flow/step-4-share.png)
